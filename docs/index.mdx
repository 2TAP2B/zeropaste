features:
  - title: encrypted in the browser
    icon: 🔒
    details: AES-256-GCM via WebCrypto. The key lives in the URL fragment or your head - it never reaches the server, the logs or the database (there is no database).
  - title: shares that die
    icon: ⏳
    details: every paste carries a ttl from one minute to 30 days. Burn-after-reading deletes a paste the moment the first reader decrypts it.
  - title: files, images, bundles
    icon: 📎
    details: drop files or clipboard-paste screenshots. Bundles upload in resumable chunks, show thumbnails and open in a lightbox - all encrypted like the text.
  - title: reverse shares
    icon: 📥
    details: need something back? Create a drop link, friends upload through it, you collect the arrivals on your dashboard. Their content stays invisible to the server.
  - title: passkey accounts
    icon: 🔑
    details: optional and local - a passkey unlocks your private dashboard of active shares. Anonymous pasting stays the default.
  - title: one binary
    icon: 🐹
    details: a single Go binary, stdlib only, no database, no CDN calls. Runs anywhere from a scratch container to a Raspberry Pi.
