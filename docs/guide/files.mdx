# files and images

The compose card takes text and files side by side: drag and drop, click to browse, or paste an image straight from the clipboard.

## bundles

Files travel as an encrypted **bundle**:

- one key per share (random or derived from your passphrase), a fresh IV per blob;
- a manifest (filenames, mime types, sizes, thumbnails) is encrypted alongside the files - **the server never learns a filename**;
- everything rides the same `/p/<id>` link as a text paste; readers see the note plus a file list.

The practical ceiling is ~2 GiB (the browser encrypts in memory).

## chunked, resumable upload

Large bundles leave the browser in chunks (default **8 MiB** per request):

- the upload session survives a lost connection - re-PUT a chunk to resume;
- the sender can cancel mid-flight; the temp session is deleted;
- a session that never finishes is swept after 2 hours;
- readers download ranges on demand - a 100 MiB bundle only moves the chunks you open.

## image previews

Decodable images get a thumbnail encrypted next to the file: the card shows the small thumbnail first and decrypts the full image only when you open the lightbox or hit **Save**. Old bundles without thumbnails fall back to eager decryption for small images.

## limits

| knob | env | default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| total ciphertext per bundle | `MAX_UPLOAD` | 100 MiB (up to 64 GiB) |
| per-request chunk | `CHUNK` | 8 MiB (64 KiB..64 MiB) |

`CHUNK` must stay at or below `MAX_UPLOAD` - the server refuses to boot otherwise. Both are editable in the [admin config](/guide/configuration) after a save + reboot.
